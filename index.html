Thirty years of moving Indian plants from manual to measured

India's chemical and pharmaceutical plants are changing how they run: from manual batches to automated, validated and connected operation. Pretech has engineered that shift since 1996, one plant at a time.

What began as a partnership firm in Pune is now a private limited company with three business verticals: process systems (EPC), process automation (projects) and control panels (manufacturing), plus an Industry 4.0 practice. We are an authorised system integrator for Schneider Electric and also supply Emerson automation systems.

PS1

Process systems

Engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) of turnkey pilot plants, bulk material handling, weighing and batching systems and skid-mounted assemblies. Process equipment, mechanical structure, piping, electrical and instrumentation come under one contract.

Pilot plants

Pilot-scale process plants engineered, manufactured, tested and supplied as an EPC contract, built from the customer's basic process concept. A pilot plant is where a lab process proves it can scale, so the vessels, structure, piping, electricals and instrumentation arrive as one engineered package.

AU2

Automation

Industrial process automation on a turnkey basis, from concept to commissioning: process instrumentation, the automation control system and the electrical system.

Turnkey projects, control and instrumentation

Field instruments to control room, under one scope.

  • Design, manufacture, supply, erection and commissioning of process instrumentation and PLC, SCADA or DCS
  • Detailed engineering, drawings and documentation to standard engineering practice
  • Design, sizing, selection and supply of sensors, transmitters and actuated valves
  • PLC, SCADA or DCS with control and marshalling cabinets, computers, consoles and networking
  • Cables, cable trays and installation hardware
  • Engineers, supervisors and technicians deputed for site installation
  • Factory acceptance test, calibration, commissioning, production trials, training and site acceptance test

Turnkey projects, control systems

The same scope as a control and instrumentation project, without the field instrumentation and site installation.

  • Authorised system integrator of Schneider Electric for their PLC, HMI, SCADA and DCS range
  • Architectures from a single non-redundant controller to hot-standby, multi-controller distributed configurations on the Schneider EcoStruxure platform
  • Control system design based on the team's domain experience in chemicals and life sciences

Engineering and project management services

Our engineering team working as your automation consultant, from concept to commissioning.

  • P&ID preparation with instrument symbols and tag numbers
  • Field instrument design and selection for the process conditions
  • Material requisitions with detailed specifications for instruments, the control system and UPS
  • Control system concept and basic system architecture
  • Typical hook-up schematics, bills of material, cable and cable tray specifications
  • Instrument air requirement calculation

  • Inspection at supplier works before dispatch, then supervision of installation, pre-commissioning and commissioning
